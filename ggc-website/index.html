How certification works

From application to certificate.

A clear four-stage path with named auditors, fixed milestones and no surprises.

Application & quote

Tell us your scheme, sites and supply chain model. We define the audit scope and send a transparent quotation.

Stage 1 · Readiness

Document review and site readiness check to find gaps before the main audit.

Stage 2 · Assessment

On-site audit, including field inspection, stakeholder consultation, worker interviews and evidence review.

Certification & surveillance

After any non-conformities are closed, an independent reviewer makes the certification decision. Annual surveillance audits keep the certificate valid.

What we assess

Three pillars of sustainability.

Social

Labour rights, fair wages, community engagement, free, prior and informed consent (FPIC), and the protection of smallholders and workers.

Environment

No deforestation, protection of high-conservation-value (HCV) areas and peatland, greenhouse gas reduction, and responsible water and chemical management.

Safety

Occupational health and safety systems, safe handling of agrochemicals, and emergency preparedness across every operating unit.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is MSPO certification mandatory in Malaysia?

Yes. MSPO certification is mandatory for oil palm growers and processors in Malaysia. GGC is accredited by the Department of Standards Malaysia to carry out MSPO audits.

Can we combine MSPO and RSPO audits?

Where scheme rules allow it, we coordinate combined or back-to-back audits, so you face less disruption and pay less than with separate audit teams.

How long does certification take?

It depends on your scope, number of sites and readiness. We give you a milestone plan with your quotation so you know every date in advance.

How do stakeholders raise a concern?

Anyone can submit comments on an upcoming audit, or a complaint, through the contact form below. Every submission is logged and handled impartially.
